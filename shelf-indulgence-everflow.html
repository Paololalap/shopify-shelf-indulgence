The Four Pillars

The Elements of Everflow

Each student bound to a force older than memory. Each carrying a secret the academy never warned them about.

Element I

Water

"To flow is not to be weak — it is to be inevitable."

The Water student learns to read the tides of emotion — their own and others'. Fluid, adaptive, dangerously perceptive. The element that erodes mountains without ever raising its voice.

Element II

Fire

"Destruction and creation share the same breath."

The Fire student burns with passion that cannot be contained — and sometimes, cannot be controlled. Creation and ruin are two sides of the same flame. Everflow must teach them which to wield.

Element III

Earth

"Roots go deeper than memory. So does truth."

The Earth student carries the weight of history — of things buried and forgotten. Steadfast, stubborn, and ancient in their instincts. What grows from them cannot easily be uprooted.

Element IV

Air

"You cannot hold the wind. You can only learn to move with it."

The Air student exists between moments — quick, invisible, impossible to catch. They carry secrets the way the wind carries seed: without intention, and without mercy.

The Story

Everflow

The Balance-Born Saga · Year One

Four students. Four elements. One fragile balance.

Everflow was meant to train them. Instead, it might destroy them.

Because when the elements awaken… the truth rises with them.

When four unlikely students are selected to attend Everflow — the most prestigious elemental academy in the realm — each believes they know what they're signing up for. Rigorous training. Ancient knowledge. A future shaped by mastery.

But Everflow keeps secrets. And some secrets have been buried long enough to grow teeth. As the balance between elements begins to fracture, each student must confront not just their power, but the truth of why they were really chosen.

The Mind Behind the Stories

Meet Jessie Heart

Author · Teacher · Storyteller

"As a child, she would gaze out car windows, weaving fantastical tales from the world rushing by. That love for storytelling never left her."

Having been a passionate school teacher for 24 years, wife, and proud mother of four beautiful children, Jessie continues to inspire others through her words and creativity. A devoted lover of books, history, and all things royal, she finds joy in crafting stories and getting lost in new worlds.

Everflow is her debut novel — a world built in stolen moments, between lesson plans and bedtime stories, where four students learn what it truly means to carry an element within them.
